# 18. Legal pages and privacy

## Why

- The legal notice and privacy policy are course texts; a public app in Germany needs an Impressum (DDG § 5) and a privacy policy (GDPR Art. 13) that name the real processing (Supabase, hosting, Sentry)
- Interview angle: "What does GDPR mean for a frontend developer?", "Do you need a cookie banner?"

## Scope

1. Impressum and Datenschutzerklärung in German first, English below, for a private portfolio project (legal review is in `todo-before-live.md`)
2. Processing record in `docs/privacy/processing-record.md`: what data, where (Supabase region, host, Sentry EU), how long, legal basis
3. ADR: no tracking and no non-essential cookies/storage → no consent banner; what is stored in the browser (Supabase session in local storage — technically necessary) is named in the policy
4. Retention: demo (anonymous) users and their workspaces deleted after a fixed period (scheduled function or documented job)
5. **Data export (GDPR Art. 20)**: "Download my data" in the account settings (9): a JSON file with the user's profile, memberships, the tasks they created, their comments (17) — an RPC `export_my_data()` scoped by `auth.uid()` (no service role needed, so no Edge Function), with an integration test that a second user's data never appears
6. The processing record and the policy cover comments and activity (17); feature 21 adds notifications to both
7. Links from login, sign-up and the shell; the sign-up form links the privacy policy

## Acceptance criteria

- Every service that receives personal data is named in the policy and the processing record; the demo cleanup runs and has a test
