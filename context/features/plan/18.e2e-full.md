# 18. Full E2E suite

## Why

- Features 4–17 each added their journeys; this feature closes the gaps and makes the suite a reliable regression net
- Interview angle: "What do you test end to end and what not?", "Flaky tests?"

## Scope

1. Inventory of pages, routes and journeys against the suite; add what no feature owned (404, deep links, invitations decline/leave, account deletion, language switch, mobile viewport flows)
2. Page objects or fixtures where they remove real duplication
3. Parallel runs with isolated users/workspaces; retries only in CI with traces; zero known flaky tests
4. axe checks on every main page (from 12)

## Acceptance criteria

- Every route has at least one journey; the suite runs green 10 times in a row in CI

- Plan: opus
- Implementation: sonnet
- architecture-reviewer: opus
