Privacy Policy

We take the protection of your personal data seriously and treat your personal data as confidential and in compliance with the statutory data protection regulations, as well as this privacy policy. This privacy policy explains what personal data we collect through Join, and for what purpose we use it.

1. Responsible party

The responsible party within the meaning of data protection law is the student team behind Join, developed as part of a web development bootcamp at the Developer Akademie. Contact details can be found in the Legal Notice.

2. What data we collect

When you register for Join, we collect and store the following personal data: your first name, last name, email address, and password (stored encrypted). If you choose to use the guest login, no personal data is stored beyond your current browser session.

Additionally, if you create contacts or tasks within Join, the information you enter (e.g. contact names, email addresses, phone numbers, task descriptions) is stored so the application can provide its core functionality to you.

3. Purpose of processing

Your data is processed exclusively to provide the functionality of Join — that is, to let you log in, manage contacts, and organize tasks on your kanban board. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the application.

4. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on servers provided by our backend infrastructure provider and is protected using industry-standard technical and organizational security measures. As Join is an educational project, we cannot guarantee the same level of availability, reliability, or security as a commercial product.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Your data is only accessible to the students maintaining Join for development and support purposes.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you at any time. You can update your contact information directly within Join, or request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us using the details in the Legal Notice.

7. Cookies and local storage

Join uses your browser's local and session storage to keep you logged in and to remember basic preferences (such as guest-session state). We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes to Join or applicable law. The current version always applies.
